Controller

Turn pairing on, then put the controller in pairing mode. One controller is used at a time: disconnect the current one before pairing another.

  • Xbox Wireless Controller: switch it on and hold the Pair button for 3 seconds, until the Xbox button flashes quickly.
  • DualSense (PS5): with the controller off, hold Create and PS together until the light bar flashes.

A controller remembers a single Bluetooth host: pairing it here replaces its pairing with the PC or the console. Once paired, it reconnects when you switch it on with the Xbox or PS button.
